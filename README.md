# NOVLY Korea: Channel Operations Playbook (Sample)

A three-page sample playbook for a fictional consumer electronics brand selling through six channels and 42 partners in Korea.

- [English PDF](NOVLY_Channel_Ops_Playbook_EN.pdf)
- [한국어 PDF](NOVLY_Channel_Ops_Playbook_KR.pdf)

## What's inside
1. Operating rhythm: weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly, and half-year reviews, with the numbers and decisions for each
2. Partner tiering (six weighted criteria with scoring anchors) and incentive guidelines: menu, cap, payment basis, exception approval with Finance and Legal
3. One-page monthly executive dashboard with next-quarter outlook, plus metric definitions and data flow
4. GTM readiness gates from T-8 weeks to T+4 weeks
5. Process improvement and controls: incentive claim workflow, quarterly audit, segregation of duties
6. 90-day rollout (diagnose, pilot, scale and train) and regional/HQ alignment
7. How each mechanism maps to my Shopee experience (2020–2024)

NOVLY is a fictional brand and all numbers are fictional data. Not affiliated with any real company or partner.
